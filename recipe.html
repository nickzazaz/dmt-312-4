🍕 Ultimate Pizza Dip Recipe

Ingredients

FOR THE PULL-APART BREAD

  • 16.3 oz can Grands biscuit dough, 8 count
  • ¼ cup melted butter
  • 2 tsp parmesan cheese, grated
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning

FOR THE PIZZA DIP

  • 8 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 1 cup ricotta cheese
  • 3 tsp Italian seasoning, divided
  • 1 cup marinara sauce, or pizza sauce
  • 1 ½ cups mozzarella cheese, grated
  • 8 slices pepperoni, quartered

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Preheat the oven to 350°F.
  2. Step 2: Cut each biscuit in half, then roll each half of dough into a ball.
  3. Step 3: Combine the melted butter, parmesan cheese, and 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning in a bowl.
  4. Step 4: Roll each of the balls of biscuit dough in the seasoned butter, then arrange them around the edge of a greased large cast iron skillet.
  5. Step 5: In a large bowl, use an electric mixer to combine the cream cheese, ricotta cheese and 2 teaspoons Italian seasoning.
  6. Step 6: Spread the cream cheese mixture in the bottom of the skillet in the middle of the balls of dough.
  7. Step 7: Top with the marinara sauce, shredded mozzarella cheese and remaining 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning. Top with pepperonis.
  8. Step 8: Place in the oven and bake for 25-30 minutes.
